What the Post Writing Engine does

It writes social media posts for you, using the information in your AI Content Kit, so the posts sound like you and talk about your work. You choose what you want a post to say and the Engine drafts it.

Why it needs your own API key

The Engine writes your posts with Claude, through Anthropic’s API. That is a separate service from your Claude plan (Pro, Max or any other), so the API key is your own and is billed to you separately by Anthropic. Your Claude plan does not cover it.

How to get your key

  1. Open Anthropic’s guide, how to get an API key, and follow it to sign in or create an API account.
  2. Add credit to the API account. The account needs credit before a key will write anything.
  3. Create a key and copy it.
  4. Paste the key into the Post Writing Engine when it asks for it.

Where your key is kept

Your key stays in your browser, and is kept there only if you choose to remember it. It is not part of your kit files, so keep a note of it somewhere safe in case you need to enter it again.
