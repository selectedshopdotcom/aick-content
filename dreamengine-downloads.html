AI Content Kit: Skills Downloads

Claude Skills that work alongside your AI Content Kit. Download the ones you want and add them to Claude on your desktop. Each skill is a folder with a single SKILL.md inside.

How to install a skill

  1. These run in Claude on the desktop app, in Cowork, with a Claude Pro subscription or higher.
  2. For a skill below, make a new folder on your computer named exactly as shown under its download button, for example aick-book-editing-for-authors-skill.
  3. Click download to save the skill's SKILL.md into that folder. The file must stay named SKILL.md.
  4. Zip that folder, so SKILL.md sits at the top level inside the zip.
  5. In the Claude desktop app, go to Customize, then Skills. Select the + button, then Create skill, then Upload a skill, and choose the zip file.
  6. Repeat for each skill you want. Once added, a skill switches on when you ask for what it does.

Before you start, put your manuscript in a folder on your computer and use Add folder in Cowork to connect it. Claude reads your manuscript from there and saves its working files alongside it; your manuscript file is never edited.

Book Editing

Opens with a choice of a standard edit, a deep developmental edit, or a proofread, then works one chapter at a time and gives you a numbered list of issues to action yourself, each with the phrase concerned, the problem, and a suggested fix. A running notes file lets you pause and resume anytime.

Book Formatting

Turns your finished manuscript into a print PDF, an EPUB, or both. It builds a first version from sensible defaults, takes your style changes against the real thing, and finishes by checking the margins against the print service's rules for your final page count.
