How it works

Three steps. Then Claude knows your role.

No technical setup. Fill in the form for your role once and the work is done.

01

Choose your role and answer the questions

Pick from six maritime crew roles. The app opens a guided form built specifically for how your role operates. Your seafaring story, your voice, your vessel, your experience, and the records that matter in your work.

02

Generate your context files

Hit generate. The app writes a structured set of files into your Claude Cowork folder. Your professional identity becomes a permanent part of how Claude works for you.

03

Claude knows your role in every session

Claude reads your files automatically from that point on. Update your answers as your career develops and regenerate. Your context grows with you.
