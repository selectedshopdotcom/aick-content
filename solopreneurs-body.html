How it works

Three steps. Then Claude knows you.

No technical setup. Fill in the form once and the work is done.

01

Choose your industry and answer the questions

Pick from twelve solopreneur industries. The app branches into a guided form built specifically for how you work. Your voice, your brand, your clients, your content pillars. Every field earns its place.

02

Generate your context files

Hit generate. The app writes a structured set of files into your Claude Cowork folder. Your business context becomes a permanent part of how Claude operates for you.

03

Claude knows you in every session

From that point on, Claude reads your files automatically at the start of every session. No copying, no pasting, no repeating yourself. Update your answers any time and regenerate.
