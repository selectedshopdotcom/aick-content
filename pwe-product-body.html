What it does

You choose what you want a post to say. The Engine drafts it using the information in your kit: who you are, your work, and how you like to come across. It saves the posts in your kit folder, so you can edit them and post them wherever you like.

Where it comes from

It is made for every AI Content Kit app. It comes in the kit: when you build your kit, the Engine is in the kit folder, ready to open with one click. There is nothing extra to buy from selectedshop.

What you need

  • A computer running Windows or macOS, with Chrome or Edge to open the Engine. It does not work on a phone.
  • Your AI Content Kit.
  • Your own Anthropic API key with credit on it. The Engine writes with Claude through Anthropic’s API, which is billed to you by Anthropic separately from any Claude plan. Your Claude plan does not cover it. The API-key page shows you how to get one.

Your information

Only the parts of your kit needed to write a post are sent to Claude. Your finances, contacts and licence details never are. Your API key stays in your browser and is kept only if you choose to remember it. The Engine saves only into its own posts folder inside your kit.
