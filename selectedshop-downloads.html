AI Content Kit Downloads

Claude Skills that work alongside your AI Content Kit. Find your kit below, download the skills you want, and add them to Claude on your desktop. Each skill is a folder with a single SKILL.md inside.

How to install a skill

  1. These run in Claude on the desktop app, in Cowork, with a Claude Pro subscription or higher.
  2. For a skill below, make a new folder on your computer named exactly as shown under its download button.
  3. Click download to save the skill's SKILL.md into that folder. The file must stay named SKILL.md.
  4. In the Claude desktop app, go to Settings, then Capabilities, then Skills. Choose Add skill and select the folder you made.
  5. Repeat for each skill you want. Once added, a skill switches on when you ask for what it does.

Before you start, put your working files in a folder on your computer and use Add folder in Cowork to connect it. Claude reads from there and saves its working files alongside them; your own files are never edited.
